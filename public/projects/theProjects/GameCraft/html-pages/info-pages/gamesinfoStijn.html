    Description:

    In Frogger you play as a frog trying to cross a road with cars
    and a river with crocodiles , logs and more.
    You have to avoid the cars and other dangers.
    The goal is to get the highest score by getting all the frogs to the other side
    to clear the stage and get points.

    TECHNOLOGIEEN
    HTML 5
    CSS 3
    JS

    Rules:

    try to get to the other side of the road and river withouth dying
    Up arrow (↑) → Move upward
    Down arrow (↓) → Move backward
    Left arrow (←) → Move left
    Right arrow (→) → Move right

    KEY FEATURES
    Leaderboard & Login
    Progression saving
    Movement
    & timed progress
